## 2. What the SERP looks like (US, "field sales app" and "field sales software")
Both start with an **AI Overview** that cites ~10 sources, then organic results. Page types, not just brands:
- Vendor feature/landing pages: Outfield (`/field_sales_software_and_app`, `/sales_rep_tracking_app`), Repmove (`/field-sales-app/`), SimplyDepo, InSitu Sales, Creatio marketplace listing.
- Vendor "best apps" listicles (self-ranking): SPOTIO `/blog/best-apps-for-sales-reps/` (appears at 3 and 6; Ahrefs est. 510 visits), Repmove review-grades, LeadBeam, croclub, lead-mapper.
- Reddit thread (r/SalesOperations, DR 95): positions 2-4.
- Review directory: Gartner Peer Insights (`/reviews/market/field-sales-software`).
- Glossary pages: SalesRabbit, Apollo "what is field sales software".
- People-also-ask: "Is there a free app that can track field employees?", "What is the best app for salespeople?", "What's the difference between CRM and FSM?"
Implications: ranking requires (a) one strong commercial page per head term, (b) presence in third-party review/listicle surfaces (Gartner/G2/Capterra-type profiles, Reddit), (c) AI-Overview-friendly factual passages (plain definitions, feature/limits tables). A Hemmiles self-ranked "best apps" article is possible but is a Loop 21 decision (credibility, competitor-trademark rules).
