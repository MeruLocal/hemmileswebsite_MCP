## 2. Comparison findings

### F1 (high) Hemmiles' India price is much higher than the listed field-force tools
Arithmetic from published figures (units differ, so treat as order of magnitude, not like-for-like):
- Hemmiles Starter USD 39 per user per month (INR 3,500; monthly) vs TrackoField USD 8 (quarterly term): 39 / 8 = **4.9x**.
- Hemmiles annual USD 32 (INR 2,900) vs TrackoField annual USD 5: 32 / 5 = **6.4x**.
- Spoors USD 12-22 per month: its price basis (per user vs per account) is not stated, so no ratio is claimed.
Hemmiles' India price equals its US price in USD terms (INR 3,500 is about USD 39 at the site's own conversion), so **the India page sells at US price points**. These India vendors bundle attendance, live tracking and sometimes orders; Hemmiles sells visit evidence plus CRM connection. Price premium needs a clear value story or a revised India packaging decision (Q72). Loop 20's scoped claim stays: Hemmiles is not "lowest price" in India.

### F2 (high) Scope mismatch with India buyers' expectations
India field-force vendors commonly include: attendance (Trackolap, TrackoField, Spoors free, Lystloc, PepUpSales, FieldBlaze), live tracking (all but Spoors), order booking (PepUpSales, FieldBlaze, Trackolap FMCG), beat plans (FieldBlaze, Trackolap FMCG), payments/collections (Trackolap, PepUpSales). Hemmiles claims none of attendance, live tracking, orders or beat-plan *software* (only beat-plan guides). India keyword set (Loop 10) is built on "field force tracking", "salesman tracking", "sales tracking app", which attract attendance-and-tracking intent. This sharpens Loop 11's boundary lines and the "no surveillance" positioning (Q51).

### F3 (medium) Free and low-friction entry is common
FieldBlaze has a free plan (up to 5 users), Spoors a 14-day trial, Trackolap a no-card trial; Hemmiles offers a 14-day no-card trial and no free plan, with 3-user minimums. A pilot offer (3-10 users, 4 weeks, success criteria; Loop 28 experiment 5) may suit India buyers.

### F4 (medium) Trust signals Hemmiles lacks
Trackolap shows customer logos/names, an ISO badge and a sub-processor list; Lystloc SOC 2/ISO badges; Spoors an enterprise count. Hemmiles shows none (Loop 27). India SERP competitors with DR 2-10 still rank, so trust gaps do not prevent ranking but do affect conversion.

### F5 (low) Pricing presentation patterns
Several vendors hide prices (PepUpSales, Lystloc toggle, FieldBlaze base prices missing, Trackolap per-product pages); Hemmiles publishes full prices and a plan matrix, a transparency advantage if the numbers are competitive.
