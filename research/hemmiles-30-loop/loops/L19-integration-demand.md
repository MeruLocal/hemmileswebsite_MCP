## 4. Conditional page briefs (publish only after gate G18 passes for that CRM)
**"Hemmiles + <CRM>" page (template)**: purpose = remove the "will it work with our CRM?" objection; one page per proven connector, no placeholders.
Required content: connection type; supported records and direction (table from Loop 18); field mapping; ownership rule; photos/notes handling; retry behaviour; limitations; plan (Professional+) and setup effort; screenshot or short demo of T6/T8; last tested date and app version; FAQ.
Internal links: /integrations (parent), /features/follow-ups, /pricing (Professional), /demo.
Do NOT: state "native/certified/official partner" unless true; promise real-time sync without proof; list CRMs "supported" because customers can use CSV.
Metric: demo requests that mention the CRM; assisted conversions; AI-answer citations (Loop 26). Not organic rank.
