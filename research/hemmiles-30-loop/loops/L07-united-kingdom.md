## 3. UK-specific buyer / regulatory points (secondary sources; confirm with counsel)
- ICO final workplace-monitoring guidance (published 3 Oct 2023 per law-firm summaries): choose the least intrusive method; avoid using monitoring data beyond its stated purpose; a DPIA recommended for workplace monitoring; extra care for home workers and for special-category data; Article 22 applies to solely automated decisions with significant effects (e.g. pay set purely from automated monitoring).
- Fit with Hemmiles: the product's event-based check-in/check-out (not continuous tracking) aligns with the "least intrusive" test; this strengthens the planned "GPS check-in without surveillance" content - **but only if** the actual product behaviour is verified (Loop 11: what is recorded, when, who sees it, retention). No retention facts exist (privacy page is a template).
- UK buyers commonly ask for: UK/EU data hosting location, a DPA, sub-processor list, and clear privacy notice. The privacy page is unfinished (Loop 3 F4); sub-processors, retention and legal identity are blank. This is a **conversion blocker for UK B2B**, not just SEO.
