## 3. UAE
- Live `/ae`: same "field sales, field service, distribution, collections and medical sales teams across the United Arab Emirates". Prices in dirhams: AED 145 / 180 / 365 monthly; AED 120 / 150 / 300 annual.
- Ahmedabad sample data 7 times; no VAT or Arabic mention; page is English only (no RTL).
- Buyer readiness: UAE buyers frequently ask about local hosting, VAT invoicing (5% VAT is typical in the UAE; unverified for this business), Arabic UI/support, and a local entity/contact for contracts. Hemmiles shows none of these.
- Readiness: **LOW**. English-only is acceptable for many B2B buyers but search demand for the product category is only ~50/month.
