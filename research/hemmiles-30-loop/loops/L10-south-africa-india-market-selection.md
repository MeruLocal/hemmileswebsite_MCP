## 4. Selection (maximum two initial execution markets)
1. **India (execute).** Only market where demand, vocabulary and page are already aligned, with contracted INR pricing. Lowest cost to improve existing pages.
2. **United States (execute with gates).** Largest measured demand and the commercially valuable terms (CPC $6-25), but pages are not localised and claims/privacy are unfinished. Gates: privacy/terms completed (owner task), CRM-connection and offline claims verified (Loops 11, 18), US-vocabulary page updates (no new URLs), baseline wired (Loop 2).
3. **United Kingdom (reserve, conditional).** Take only if the service-team hypothesis ("field service app" UK 250, AU 200, SG 90) is supported in Loop 15 and UK data-protection facts exist.
4. **Defer, no work:** CA, AU, NZ, IE, SG, AE, ZA and all long-tail markets.

Honest caveat: the brief put US/CA/UK first for research. The data confirms the US as the demand leader but does **not** support Canada or the UK as execution markets now. India is a decision for the founder: it is where the site is already strongest, and it conflicts with the brief's "US, Canada, UK first" ordering only in sequence, not in analysis.

## 5. Expansion conditions (to move a deferred market up)
- Real inbound leads or trials from that country, or GSC impressions >= a threshold the founder sets (not defined here).
- Verified data-protection facts (hosting, retention, DPA).
- Localised, non-India sample data and billing-currency wording.
- A product-supported use case that matches local demand (e.g. service in AU/UK/SG).

## 6. Decisions
1. Recommended execution markets: **India + US** (UK reserve). Founder to confirm (Q26).
2. No work on CA/AU/NZ/IE/SG/AE/ZA/long-tail beyond global clean-ups (sample data, currency sentence).
3. Re-score after Loops 11, 18 and 28 when proof and measurement exist.
