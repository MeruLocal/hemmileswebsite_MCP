## 3. Canadian requirements that differ from the US (secondary sources; confirm with counsel)
- **Privacy regime depends on the employer's jurisdiction**: federal PIPEDA mainly for federally regulated employers; Quebec, BC and Alberta have their own private-sector laws (WebSearch summary of commentary).
- **Quebec**: Law 25 / regulator guidance (Oct 2023, per search result) expects **express consent** for technologies that identify or locate individuals, and a documented necessary purpose. Location capture at check-in/check-out (event-based) is a better posture than continuous tracking; this aligns with Hemmiles' "GPS check-in without surveillance" article. Needs legal verification before any Quebec-facing claim.
- **Language**: Quebec buyers may need French UI/support/legal pages. Hemmiles has no French product or site content (live `/fr` is a French *country* page in English, noindex). Treat Quebec as out of scope unless a real Quebec buyer appears.
- Taxes: GST/HST/PST/QST vary by province; the page's generic "local tax on top" line is safe; do not state provincial rates.
- Unit/format: km, CAD, date format dd/mm vs mm/dd: only matters in sample screenshots (see Ahmedabad issue).
